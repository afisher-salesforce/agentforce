Executive Enablement

Agentic AI for Siemens DISW: From Strategy to Scaled Execution

This workshop is designed for Siemens Digital Industries Software IT leadership to evaluate how Embedded AI, Agentforce, Data 360, Headless 360, and Observability combine into one governed architecture that accelerates idea-to-production outcomes.

Executive Purpose

Establish a shared operating model for AI adoption that keeps IT in control while delivering measurable business value faster than custom-only approaches.

Session Outcome

A practical enablement path for DISW teams to move from concept, to architecture, to deployment and ongoing governance using Salesforce as the trusted AI execution layer.

External Research

Siemens DISW Context and Alignment Signals

Public and account-level research indicates DISW is balancing SaaS transition, post-M&A integration, and AI-led growth targets—creating strong demand for governed automation and unified data context.

Business and Transformation Signals

  • DISW is transitioning from perpetual licensing toward SaaS/subscription under Xcelerator.
  • Recent Altair and Dotmatics integrations increase urgency for customer data unification.
  • Digital business growth and AI investment are high, but margin and complexity pressures remain.
  • IT leadership is focused on platform governance, rationalization, and faster value realization.

Implications for the Session

  • Anchor the conversation in architecture decisions, not isolated feature demonstrations.
  • Show how governed AI execution reduces long-term maintenance burden and operational risk.
  • Emphasize measurable outcomes: resolution rates, cycle-time reduction, and workload deflection.
Domain 01

Embedded AI

Embedded AI gives DISW teams an immediate productivity layer within existing workflows, while preserving governance controls and human oversight.

Executive Talking Points

  • Shift teams from low-value manual orchestration to higher-order design and decision work.
  • Use embedded assistants to standardize responses, recommendations, and operational playbooks.
  • Position IT as an enablement center that governs models, prompts, and policy guardrails.
Domain 02

Agentforce

Agentforce turns natural language intent into deployable agents with structured topics, actions, and guardrails for enterprise-grade execution.

Executive Talking Points

  • Move from “AI pilot” to production workflows with repeatable deployment patterns.
  • Balance build vs buy using governed platform primitives instead of fragmented custom stacks.
  • Create agent portfolios mapped to DISW priorities: support, entitlement, quoting, and partner operations.
Domain 03

Data 360

Data 360 provides the unified context fabric for grounded AI decisions across Salesforce and external systems, including SAP and product telemetry sources.

Executive Talking Points

  • Ground agent outputs in current customer, entitlement, and adoption context—not disconnected data snapshots.
  • Support zero-copy patterns to avoid unnecessary replication and new data-silo creation.
  • Improve trust and actionability by unifying context before inference and automation decisions.
Domain 04

Headless 360

Headless 360 enables Salesforce capabilities on non-Salesforce digital surfaces, turning portals and apps into governed AI engagement channels without duplicative backend sprawl.

Executive Talking Points

  • Deliver agentic experiences in DISW-preferred interfaces while retaining platform trust controls.
  • Reduce integration and maintenance overhead from custom middleware-heavy patterns.
  • Support decoupled front-end innovation while centralizing execution, data policy, and governance.
Domain 05

Observability

Observability is the control layer that makes agentic systems production-ready by exposing health, quality, escalation patterns, and improvement opportunities over time.

Executive Talking Points

  • Track outcomes with business and IT metrics: containment, escalation, quality, and cycle time.
  • Operationalize an agentic DevOps lifecycle: build, test, deploy, observe, and optimize.
  • Build leadership confidence with transparent governance and continuous quality scoring.
