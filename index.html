Context

The GenAI Divide and Why Execution Matters

Despite $30-40B in enterprise GenAI spend, 95% of organizations see zero measurable return. Only 5% of pilots create real value, largely because systems fail to learn, adapt, or fit real workflows, not because of model quality or regulation.

What Differentiates Winners

  • Demand process-specific customization tied to real workflow constraints.
  • Judge tools on business outcomes, not model novelty or demo quality.
  • Favor external partnership execution patterns over internal-only build models when speed and scale are required.
Executive Enablement

Agentic AI for Siemens DISW: From Strategy to Scaled Execution

Expose Siemens DISW IT leaders to Salesforce AI capabilities beyond CPQ+, including Vibe Coding, Agentforce, Data 360, Agent Fabric, Headless 360, and Observability.

Session Objective

Purpose and Outcomes

Executive Purpose

Show how DISW can accelerate from idea to production using Salesforce-native AI capabilities with enterprise-grade governance and delivery controls.

Executive Enablement - Session Outcome

Align on immediate next steps for enablement, execution, and a Center of Excellence that improves consumption and business impact under the Siemens SELA.

What Must Be True at Session End

  • IT leadership understands Salesforce AI capabilities, including build and governance implications.
  • Leaders understand where Vibe Coding and Agentforce can accelerate time-to-value in current initiatives.
  • DISW identifies candidate business workflows for rapid AI whiteboarding and pilot design.

Executive Stakes by Persona

  • IT Strategy: show measurable business outcomes and platform-level governance confidence.
  • Security: define control points for A2A and MCP access to operational systems.
  • Application Support: improve containment, response consistency, and service quality at scale.
  • Development and Testing: reduce build cycle time while preserving quality and release discipline.
Current Baseline

Current State, Friction Points, and Constraints

The architecture and organizational baseline creates both urgency and opportunity for a more unified AI operating model anchored on Salesforce.

Primary Pain Points

  • Current team structures are not yet optimized to deliver Salesforce AI at speed and enterprise scale.
  • Most AI stakeholders do not have a clear definition of what tools to use when and how they can work together.

Architecture and Delivery Reality

  • Multiple Salesforce orgs increase integration and governance complexity.
  • Data needed to hydrate agents is scattered across the DISW landscape.
  • There is currently no overarching governance across all AI agents being created.

Strategic Constraints

  • Competing platforms shape stakeholder expectations (Teams, Copilot, SAP, open-source AI initiatives).
  • Buy-vs-build concerns include services cost, internal capability development, and speed-to-market pressure.
  • No formal Salesforce Center of Excellence currently standardizes best practices and reusable delivery patterns.
Research Alignment

Siemens DISW Context and Alignment Signals

Public and account-level research indicates DISW is balancing SaaS transition, post-M&A integration, and AI-led growth targets, creating strong demand for governed automation and unified data context.

Business and Transformation Signals

  • DISW is transitioning from perpetual licensing toward SaaS/subscription, increasing the importance of customer retention.
  • Continuing acquisitions increase urgency for customer data unification to extract maximum value from investments.
  • Digital business growth and AI investment are high, while margin and complexity pressures remain.
  • IT leadership focus is on platform governance, rationalization, and faster value realization.

Implications for This Session

  • Anchor discussion in architecture and operating model decisions, not isolated feature demos.
  • Frame Salesforce AI as the path to execution speed inside an already-approved global platform commitment.
  • Position measurable progress around use-case adoption and capability consumption rather than customer-specific metrics.
